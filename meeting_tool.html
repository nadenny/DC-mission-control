Tips for best results

Bullet points are enough. Just type your agenda items one per line. Claude will estimate time per item, arrange them logically, and write a clean timed agenda you can copy and send.

Standing items are pre-filled with your usual opening, admin updates, and open floor. Edit or remove them — they always surround your main items.

Use the context field to flag anything sensitive: a topic that needs more time, someone who should own a particular item, or a decision you need to actually reach.

Your API key is used only in your browser. It is never stored or sent anywhere except directly to Anthropic.

Why this matters
The end-of-year problem, solved

Log it when you see it. Evaluations, recommendation letters, and admin conversations all happen months after the thing you're trying to remember. Thirty seconds now saves an hour in May.

Log the small stuff too. "Covered a class last minute" and "helped a colleague with Schoology" are exactly the things that vanish from memory but matter in aggregate.

Concerns go here too. Not everything is a win. A pattern of missed deadlines documented across the year is far more defensible than a vague impression.

Then generate a summary. Pick a person, hit Summarize, and Claude writes a clean year-in-review paragraph from every entry — ready for an evaluation or a rec letter.

Everything stays in your browser. Nothing is shared.
