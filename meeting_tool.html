Bullet points are enough. Just type your agenda items one per line. Claude will estimate time per item, arrange them logically, and write a clean timed agenda you can copy and send.

Standing items are pre-filled with your usual opening, admin updates, and open floor. Edit or remove them — they always surround your main items.

Use the context field to flag anything sensitive: a topic that needs more time, someone who should own a particular item, or a decision you need to actually reach.

Output is copy-ready. The generated agenda is formatted to paste directly into an email or Schoology post.

Your API key is used only in your browser. It is never stored or sent anywhere except directly to Anthropic.
